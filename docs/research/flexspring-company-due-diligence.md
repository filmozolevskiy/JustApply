## 5. Risks for a candidate (QA, Montreal remote)

1. **Pay opacity.** Senior QA posting has no range. Canadian market for senior Java automation is roughly **CA$80k–$110k**. US senior QA medians are much higher. Ask for CAD vs USD, bonus, RRSP/401k, and whether “stock” (mentioned on a sales Indeed ad) exists for R&D. Sources: [Built In](https://builtin.com/job/senior-qa-automation-specialist/10545636), salary tables in §2.
2. **You cannot triangulate culture from Glassdoor.** Only company-selected interviews exist. Ask for a skip-level and a peer in R&D QA about hours, on-call, and release pressure. Fast-paced language is in the JD. Source: [Built In](https://builtin.com/job/senior-qa-automation-specialist/10545636).
3. **R&D vs Professional Services.** QA is posted on the **R&D** product team. Services/integration consultants live in client UAT cycles. Confirm you will not be pulled into billable PS firefighting. Sources: [careers](https://www.flexspring.com/careers), Sri vs Sully interviews.
4. **Private, unfunded, ADP-concentrated.** No VC backstop in Tracxn. ADP Marketplace is a major go-to-market.
